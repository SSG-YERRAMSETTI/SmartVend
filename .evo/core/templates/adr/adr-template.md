## Context

The situation that forces a decision. What is true now, what constraint applies,
and why the current state is not sufficient. State facts and where they were
verified. Name the options that were genuinely considered and why the obvious
alternative was not chosen.

Do not write the decision here.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense, specifically enough that someone can
tell whether code complies with it.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it forecloses. Include the
costs honestly: operational burden, migration work, lock-in, and anything that
now requires discipline to maintain.

State the conditions under which this decision should be revisited.
